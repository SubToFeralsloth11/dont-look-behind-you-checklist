DON'T LOOK BEHIND YOU

Build checklist — every stage and feature. Checked = implemented and tested in game.

Stage 1 — Project inspection & architecture

  • Godot 4.7 project created (renderer, physics, audio buses, input map)
  • Folder architecture (scenes/scripts/assets/audio/levels/data/docs)
  • Target machine & 60 FPS acceptance test defined
  • Headless test runner

Stage 2 — Player & interaction system

  • First-person controller (walk, sprint, crouch, head bob, lean-free camera)
  • Stamina & breathing-driven sprint
  • Interaction raycast + small prompt
  • Doors (hinged, physical, lockable)
  • Inventory (grid GUI, items, keycards, documents)
  • Flashlight with battery
  • Hiding spots (lockers / under desks)
  • Pause menu
  • Settings menu (graphics presets, audio, mouse)

Stage 3 — Facility environment prototype

  • Modular kit: walls, floors, ceilings, doorways, corridors
  • Office wing (B1)
  • Maintenance tunnels & vent crawlspaces
  • Long sightlines that stretch into the distance
  • Theater-like auditorium room

Stage 4 — Asset acquisition & original assets

  • Asset registry (source, license, polycount, texture size)
  • CC0 PBR textures (concrete, metal, tiles, rust, wet)
  • CC0 props (furniture, equipment)
  • Original props: generators, server racks, keycard readers, CCTV cameras, elevator
  • M1911 pistol as story prop

Stage 5 — Lighting & materials

  • PBR materials with roughness / normal maps
  • Decals: dirt, water damage, blood, scratches
  • Flickering & emergency lighting
  • Volumetric fog / dust (preset dependent)
  • Power-off / power-on lighting states

Stage 6 — Environmental audio system

  • Layer 1 bed: HVAC, hum, distant machinery, structure groans
  • Layer 2 zone sounds: lab, basement water/pipes, office
  • Layer 3 player: surface-based footsteps, clothing, breathing
  • Layer 4 threat sounds
  • 3D positional audio with distance filtering
  • Occlusion (muffled through walls, clear through doorways)
  • Reverb zones per room
  • Silence system (hum drops out)
  • Dynamic mix (hiding, running, danger)
  • Sparse music stingers

Stage 7 — Breathing system

  • Breathing states: faint, distant, near, directly behind, extremely close
  • Non-repeating variations (rhythm, distance, pan, reverb)
  • Turn-around detection with consequences
  • Some breathing events alter the environment

Stage 8 — Security camera system

  • Security room with monitor bank
  • Camera switching (live feeds)
  • Remote observation & route finding
  • Impossible footage: player seen somewhere else
  • Subject 13 behind the player on camera

Stage 9 — Subject 13 model & animation

  • Original creature model (unnatural proportions, detailed surface)
  • Skeleton & animations: idle, standing, looking, walking, slow move, searching
  • Animations: sudden move, react, appear/disappear, pursuit, environmental interaction

Stage 10 — Subject 13 AI

  • State machine: idle, observing, stalking, hiding, searching, manifesting, pursuing, disappearing
  • Stops when observed / watches from distance
  • Appears where player previously was
  • Creature footsteps & breathing (3D)
  • Death sequence + checkpoint reload

Stage 11 — Environmental anomaly system

  • Authored horror event framework (ID, trigger, effect, intensity, repeat)
  • Lights off, doors, objects moving, footsteps
  • Hallway stretching, rooms changing, signs changing
  • Hallucinations

Stage 12 — Story & environmental storytelling

  • Documents / notes reader
  • Terminals & audio recordings with subtitles
  • Gore & evidence placement (balanced)
  • Survival rules discovered through notes and consequences

Stage 13 — Full level progression

  • Act 1 — The Facility (restore power, reach elevator)
  • Act 2 — B2 (three generators, breathing)
  • Act 3 — Security (CCTV, impossible footage)
  • Act 4 — The Experiment (labs, research history)
  • Act 5 — Reality Breakdown (changed areas)
  • Act 6 — The Rules
  • Objectives HUD & checkpoints / save system

Stage 14 — Final escape sequence

  • Act 7 — keycards, power, emergency elevator
  • Surface: sunlight, trees, road
  • Phone call — "Don't turn around."
  • SECURITY CAMERA 27 — ONLINE → cut to black

Stage 15 — Audio polish

  • Mix pass, variations audit, levels per zone

Stage 16 — Visual polish

  • Post-processing, color grade, decal density, screenshot review

Stage 17 — Performance optimization

  • Graphics presets Low / Medium / High / Ultra
  • Occlusion culling, visibility ranges / LOD, shadow distances
  • 60 FPS measured in heavy scenes on target machine

Stage 18 — Full playthrough testing

  • Automated start-to-end playthrough completes

Stage 19 — Bug fixing

  • All found bugs fixed

Stage 20 — Final polish & release preparation

  • macOS export preset & build
  • Credits & asset attributions
